SM ENTERPRISES STATIC WEBSITE STARTER

Files:
- index.html  Main website content
- styles.css  Responsive design and styling
- script.js   Mobile menu and email-based quotation form

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Confirm that smenterprises-laptops.in is registered and you control its DNS.
2. Replace purchase@smenterprises-laptops.in in index.html and script.js if you choose a different business email.
3. Add your real phone number, business address, company registration details, GST details (if applicable), and accurate service/warranty terms.
4. The quotation form currently opens the visitor's default email application. It does NOT send or store submissions by itself. For a hosted form, connect a service such as Formspree or a server-side form endpoint and update the form handler.
5. Brand names are displayed as examples of brands customers may enquire about. Do not claim authorised reseller status unless you have it.

DEPLOY ON CLOUDFLARE PAGES (DIRECT UPLOAD)
1. Sign in at https://dash.cloudflare.com/
2. Open Workers & Pages, choose Create, then Pages (the exact labels may change).
3. Choose direct upload / upload assets if offered, and upload the contents of this folder (index.html, styles.css, script.js) or a ZIP of the folder, following the dashboard prompts.
4. Deploy and test the temporary pages.dev URL.
5. In the deployed project, open Custom domains and add smenterprises-laptops.in. Follow Cloudflare's instructions for DNS and domain verification.
6. Add www.smenterprises-laptops.in as a custom domain too if you want the www address to work; follow the dashboard's DNS instructions.
7. Do not change MX records if this domain is already used for email. Website and email DNS records must coexist.

This is a static website starter. Domain registration, business email and any hosted enquiry-form service are separate from free static hosting.
